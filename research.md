## Research

My research focuses on problems arising in mathematical data science. I am particularly interested in designing and analyzing iterative algorithms for large-scale data and leveraging and developing tools in numerical linear algebra, signal processing, machine learning, and probability.

Below are selected recent works. A complete list of publications is available on [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&user=o4EFl3YAAAAJ).

### Kaczmarz Algorithms

- E. H. Bergou, S. Boucherouite, A. Dutta, X. Li, and A. Ma. “A Note on Randomized Kaczmarz Algorithm for Solving Doubly-Noisy Linear Systems.” Submitted (2023).
- J. Haddock, A. Ma, and E. Rebrova. “On Subsampled Quantile Randomized Kaczmarz.” To appear in the 59th Allerton Conference on Communication, Control, and Computing (2023).
- J. Haddock and A. Ma. “Greed Works: An Improved Analysis of Sampling Kaczmarz-Motzkin.” *SIAM Journal on Mathematics of Data Science* 3(1), 342–368 (2021).

- A. Ma, D. Stöger, and Y. Zhu. “Robust recovery of low-rank matrices and low-tubal-rank tensors from noisy sketches.” [arXiv:2206.00803](https://arxiv.org/abs/2206.00803), accepted in *SIAM Journal on Matrix Analysis* (2023).
