## Undergraduate Research

I am not currently taking on undergraduate students for research.

## Letters of Recommendation
I am generally able to write the strongest letters for students who have conducted research with me or interacted with me regularly for a class. 
Before requesting a letter, please have the following documents prepared:
- Your CV or résumé
- An unofficial transcript
- A list of programs, positions, or scholarships and their deadlines
  
Letters can be requested via email and must be submitted at least four weeks before the first deadline.
