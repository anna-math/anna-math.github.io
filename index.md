## About

I am an Associate Professor in the Department of Mathematics at the University of California, Irvine. My research is in mathematical data science, with an emphasis on randomized algorithms, numerical linear algebra, and methods for large-scale data.

I develop and analyze iterative methods for linear systems, tensor methods, and tools for data visualization and machine learning. See my [research page]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}) for recent papers and code.
