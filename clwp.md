## Coffee/Lunch with a Math Professor

The next CLWP will be in Fall 2023. Look out for announcements in your email!

Are you interested in graduate school, math careers in academia or industry, or life in academia? Have you ever wanted to talk to a professor with whom you have not had a class?

Sign up for lunch or coffee with a math professor. This program provides dedicated time slots to meet with professors in an informal setting and learn about research, careers in industry, applying for graduate school, and more.

Sign-ups are currently open to all undergraduate and graduate students at UC Irvine. For comments, questions, or suggestions, email [mathprofchatuci@gmail.com](mailto:mathprofchatuci@gmail.com).
